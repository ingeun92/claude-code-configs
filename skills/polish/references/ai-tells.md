# AI tells: full checklist

Tier 2 of the polish skill. SKILL.md carries §1 to §5, the detector group. This file has all 25, grouped and ordered strongest first, plus the watch-word lists.

`weak` marks a tell that needs company from other tells in the same passage, since a person may do any of them on purpose. Everything unmarked justifies an edit on one sighting.

For before/after pairs, read `examples.md` in this directory. Load it only when a call is genuinely close; the cues below settle most of them.

## A. Staging instead of stating

1. **X가 아니라 Y / not X but Y.** `단순히 X가 아니라`, `X뿐만 아니라`, `X라기보다`, or split across two sentences. The negative half names a position nobody took. State the point directly; keep the contrast only when it corrects a real assumption or both halves carry information.
2. **One-line closers, dramatic fragments.** `바로 이것이 핵심이다`, `다시 한번 읽어보자`, `That is the real win.`, `Let that sink in.`; a one-sentence paragraph restating the one above; the same closer after several sections; a row of fragments; a word in ALL CAPS or with periods between words. Cut it, or merge fragments into a sentence with a claim.
3. **Sayings that sound deep.** `결국 중요한 것은`, `본질적으로`, `진정한 의미에서`, `핵심은 ~에 있다`, `X는 Y의 Z다`, `~의 언어`, `~의 지형도`; `at its core`, `the real question is`, `what really matters`, `the heart of the matter`. Replace with the specific claim.
4. **Staged run-up.** `자, 이제 살펴보자`, `하나씩 짚어보자`, `본격적으로 들어가기 전에`, `결론부터 말하자면` (with no conclusion following); `Let's dive in`, `Here's the thing`, `Let's be honest`, `without further ado`. Delete; start at the point.
5. **Arguing with no one.** `물론 ~라는 말은 아니다`, `오해하지 말자`, `분명히 해두자면`; `Don't get me wrong`, `To be clear`, `Some might say... but`, `One might be tempted to`, `You might think... but`. Cut unless a real reader holds the objection.

## B. Rhythm by rule

6. **Forced triads.** Three items, three parallel examples, or three short facts plus a lesson, arriving in threes for completeness. Check each adds a distinct idea; merge, or develop the strongest, if not. Keep three real items when the meaning has three.
7. **Repeated openings.** Consecutive sentences starting with the same subject or connective (`그리고`, `또한`, `이는`, `he`, `she`). Merge, change the subject, or start with the action. Do not ban the word; deliberate anaphora exists.
8. **Dashes.** *Korean:* hard rule, already in SKILL.md. *English:* replace most em and en dashes with a period, comma, colon, or parentheses, including spaced dashes and `--`. Match the user's writing sample if one was given. One dash is `weak`; a text full of them is not.
9. **Stacked qualifiers.** `~할 수도 있을 것으로 보인다`, `어느 정도는 ~인 측면이 있다`; `to be fair`, `could potentially`, `might arguably`, `in some cases it may`. Keep a hedge only when the claim is genuinely uncertain. Scope statements, legal and safety notices, and real corrections stay. Ordinary hedges (`아마`, `tends to`) are human habits. `weak`
10. **Hyphenated pairs** (English only). `data-driven`, `client-facing`, `cross-functional`, `high-quality`, `real-time`, `long-term`, `end-to-end`. Hyphen before a noun, not after: `a high-quality report` but `the report is high quality`. `weak`
11. **Passive and hidden subjects.** Korean 이중피동 is covered in SKILL.md as a grammar error. Otherwise: the text hides who acts or drops the subject. Use active voice when it makes the actor clearer. `weak`

## C. Inflation and borrowed authority

The fact underneath is usually sound. Keep it, drop the dressing. Where the dressing is the only content, the sentence goes.

12. **Overused AI vocabulary.** Swap for the plain word or cut. Keep technical senses (`robust regression`, `gated recurrent unit`, `키 값`, `랜드스케이프 모드`).
13. **Inflated significance.** Say what happened instead. Also cut section headings that exist only to inflate.
14. **Vague connection.** Name the actual relationship, or keep the vagueness rather than upgrading it to causation. If the relationship is unknown, cut the sentence and ask.
15. **Shallow riders.** A trailing clause that restates the main clause instead of adding to it. Cut it, or make it a real clause with new information.
16. **Sales language.** Describe instead of selling. Keep the checkable facts, drop the adjectives around them.
17. **Borrowed authority.** Keep only named, sourced attribution. Never manufacture one: an unsourced claim stated plainly is honest, an invented citation is not.
18. **Avoiding plain verbs.** Use `~이다`, `is`, `has` where the inflated verb adds nothing.

## D. Formatting by rule

19. **Bold as decoration.** Bold on every list item's first phrase, or on emphasis words mid-sentence. Remove. Bold survives only as a real label the reader scans for, such as a term being defined. After removing bold labels from a list, check whether the list should have been prose.
20. **Decorative headings.** Emoji headers, Title Case on every heading, headings on text too short to need them. Match the document's existing convention; add none the original lacked.
21. **Curly quotes** (English only). Use straight `"` and `'` unless the original is consistently curly. Leave quotes inside code untouched.

## E. Leftovers

22. **Chatbot residue.** Delete outright.
23. **Knowledge-limit disclaimers.** Delete; this is the model narrating its own retrieval, not a fact about the subject. A genuine unknown the writer means as information stays (`창업자는 공개된 적이 없다`).
24. **Heading repeated in the first sentence.** A section opening by restating its own heading. Start with the content.
25. **Writing about the draft.** Remove drafting talk never meant for the reader. Genuine cross-references in a long document stay.

## Watch lists

**§12 Overused AI vocabulary.** KO: 다양한, 폭넓은, 풍부한, 생생한, 획기적인, 혁신적인, 극대화, 최적화 (figurative), 주목할 만한, 필수적인, 촘촘한, ~을 아우르는, ~에 다름 아니다, ~라는 점에서 의미가 있다. EN: delve, landscape (abstract), tapestry, testament, underscore, pivotal, crucial, robust (figurative), showcase, foster, intricate, interplay, key (adj.), enhance, garner, bolster, vibrant, valuable, meticulous, enduring, deep dive, align with, additionally, actually.

**§13 Inflated significance.** KO: 중요한 역할을 한다, 새 지평을 열었다, 시사하는 바가 크다, ~로 자리매김했다, 지대한 영향을 미쳤다, 밑거름이 되었다, 도전 과제에도 불구하고. EN: stands as a testament, a pivotal/crucial moment, plays a key role, marking/shaping the, underscores its importance, reflects a broader, enduring legacy, setting the stage for, evolving landscape, indelible mark, the future looks bright, a step in the right direction. Boilerplate headings: 도전 과제와 전망, 향후 계획, 시사점, Challenges and Legacy, Future Outlook, Awards and Recognition.

**§14 Vague connection.** KO: ~와 관련하여, ~와 관련되어 있다, ~와 연결되어 있다, ~와 맞닿아 있다, ~와 궤를 같이한다. EN: associated with, in association with, connected to, in connection with, linked to, tied to.

**§15 Shallow riders.** KO: trailing ~하며, ~함으로써, ~을 통해, ~는 가운데, ~와 함께 clauses that restate the main clause. EN: highlighting, underscoring, emphasizing, ensuring, reflecting, symbolizing, contributing to, cultivating, fostering, encompassing, showcasing.

**§16 Sales language.** KO: 자랑한다, ~을 선사한다, ~의 중심에 자리한, 놓칠 수 없는, 다채로운, 눈길을 사로잡는, 명실상부한, 손꼽히는. EN: boasts, vibrant, rich (figurative), profound, exemplifies, commitment to, natural beauty, nestled, in the heart of, groundbreaking (figurative), renowned, featuring, diverse array, breathtaking, must-visit, stunning.

**§17 Borrowed authority.** KO: 전문가들은 ~라고 말한다, 업계에 따르면, 일각에서는, 관계자에 따르면, ~로 알려져 있다, 호평을 받고 있다. EN: experts argue, observers have cited, industry reports, some critics, several publications, cited/featured/profiled in [outlet list], trade publications, independent coverage, active social media presence, over N followers.

**§18 Avoiding plain verbs.** KO: ~의 역할을 한다, ~로 기능한다, ~을 담당한다, ~을 의미한다, ~에 해당한다, ~을 나타낸다 where ~이다 works. EN: serves as, stands as, functions as, operates as, marks, represents, boasts, features, offers, maintains, refers to.

**§22 Chatbot residue.** KO: 도움이 되셨길 바랍니다, 궁금한 점이 있으시면 언제든 말씀해 주세요, 물론입니다!, 좋은 질문입니다, 말씀하신 대로입니다, 더 필요하신 게 있을까요, 아래와 같이 정리해 드렸습니다. EN: I hope this helps, Of course!, Certainly!, Great question!, You're absolutely right, Would you like..., Want me to...?, Should I continue?, let me know, here is a...

**§23 Knowledge-limit disclaimers.** KO: 공개된 정보에 따르면, 구체적인 내용은 알려지지 않았다, 확인된 바에 따르면, ~로 추정된다 (when the model is the one estimating), 널리 알려지지는 않았다. EN: as of [date], up to my last training update, while specific details are limited, based on available information, not publicly available, not widely documented, in the provided sources, maintains a low profile, likely [grew up / studied / began], it is believed that.

**§25 Writing about the draft.** KO: 앞서 언급했듯이, 위에서 살펴본 바와 같이, 이 글에서는 ~을 다루었다, 수정된 버전은 다음과 같다, 아래에 정리해 보았다. EN: As mentioned above, As we explored earlier, In this article we examined, Here is the revised version, I've updated the following.
